Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

This policy describes how Google user data is handled when a user authorizes Google Drive access for this personal Home Assistant installation.

Contact: y.voinorovych@gmail.com

2. Data Access

Depending on the permissions granted and the integration configuration, the application may access Google Drive files and related metadata needed to provide its configured functionality.

3. Use of Data

Google user data is used only to operate the configured Home Assistant Google Drive integration. It is not sold or used for advertising.

4. Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties for their independent use. Google may process data as necessary to provide its own services.

5. Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and tokens may be stored by Home Assistant to maintain the authorized connection. The installation owner is responsible for securing the Home Assistant system, its backups, and its credentials.

6. Retention and Revocation

Data and files are retained according to the Google Drive and Home Assistant configurations. Access can be revoked through the user's Google Account security settings. Locally stored integration credentials can be removed by removing the relevant integration and credentials from Home Assistant.

7. Changes

This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change.
